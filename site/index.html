The problem

You cannot see your own page any more.

You have looked at it several hundred times. Everyone who has an opinion about it has a different one, and none of them come with a reason attached that survives being questioned. So the backlog gets ordered by whoever spoke last, or by whatever is easiest to build.

You are not short of opinions. You are short of a priority order with reasoning attached.

What you get

Terms of the diagnosis

What this does, and what it does not.

What you are promised

  • A defensible, prioritised diagnosis of why the page underperforms.
  • A specific sequence of fixes, with the reasoning stated.
  • An artefact you can hand to a developer or contractor and have them act on it.

What you are not promised

  • A conversion rate lift, as a percentage or otherwise.
  • Any outcome that depends on you implementing the recommendations.
  • Any guarantee of revenue impact.

The audit does not include implementation or testing, so a performance claim could not be defended. Anyone who quotes you a percentage before looking at your page is guessing, and you should treat the rest of their reasoning accordingly.
